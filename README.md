StudyLens

StudyLens is an AI-powered research literature discovery and analysis platform designed to help students and researchers find relevant research papers, understand existing studies, compare research findings, and identify potential research gaps.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to summarize research papers, extract important information, compare methodologies and findings, and suggest possible future research directions.

Features

Research Paper Discovery: Search for academic papers using research topics and keywords.

Paper Relevance Analysis: Identify papers that are relevant or less relevant to the selected topic.

AI-Powered Summarization: Generate concise summaries of research papers.

Research Information Extraction: Extract methodologies, datasets, findings, and limitations.

Paper Comparison: Compare multiple research papers based on their approaches and results.

Research Gap Identification: Suggest potential research gaps based on limitations and underexplored areas in existing studies.

Future Research Suggestions: Recommend possible directions for further investigation.

Search History: Store and access previous searches and results.

Technology Stack

Frontend: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS

Backend: Python, FastAPI

Database: PostgreSQL

ORM: SQLAlchemy

Database Migrations: Alembic

AI and Text Processing: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Research Data: Academic Research APIs

Getting Started

Refer to the respective README files in the frontend/ and backend/ directories for installation and setup instructions.

General Setup

Clone or download the project repository.

Install and configure PostgreSQL.

Configure the database connection in the backend environment settings.

Install the frontend dependencies and start the React development server.

Install the backend dependencies and start the FastAPI server.

Configure the required research API credentials and AI service settings, if applicable.

Open the application in your browser and search for research papers by topic or keyword.

Project Objective

StudyLens aims to simplify the research literature review process by combining paper discovery, AI-powered analysis, multi-paper comparison, and potential research gap identification in one platform.

Note: Research gaps and future research directions generated by the platform are suggestions. Researchers should verify them against the original literature before drawing conclusions about novelty.
